Question 1 1 mark

Evaluate:

−9 + 14

Hint

Start at −9 on the number line. Adding 14 means move 14 units to the right.

Worked solution
  1. The integers have opposite signs.
  2. Subtract their absolute values: 14 − 9 = 5.
  3. Keep the sign of the number with the greater absolute value. Since 14 is positive, the result is positive.

Final answer: 5
